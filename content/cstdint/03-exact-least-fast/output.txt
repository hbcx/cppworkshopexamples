want at least 32 bits of range:
int32_t        : sizeof 4, 31 value bits
int_least32_t  : sizeof 4, 31 value bits
int_fast32_t   : sizeof 4, 31 value bits

rule of thumb:
  exact layout on disk / wire -> intN_t
  just need the range         -> int_leastN_t
  hot loop counter            -> int_fastN_t
