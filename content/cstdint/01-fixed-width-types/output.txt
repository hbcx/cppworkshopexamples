fixed-width sizes are the same everywhere:
sizeof int8_t   = 1 byte
sizeof int16_t  = 2 bytes
sizeof int32_t  = 4 bytes
sizeof int64_t  = 8 bytes

built-in widths are platform-dependent:
sizeof int   = 4 bytes
sizeof long  = 4 bytes (32 or 64 bits!)

ranges are pinned down too:
int32_t  max = 2147483647
uint32_t max = 4294967295
int64_t  max = 9223372036854775807
