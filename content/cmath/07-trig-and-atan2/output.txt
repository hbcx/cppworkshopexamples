angle of a 2D vector (radians):
atan2( 1,  1) = 0.785398   ( 45 deg)
atan2( 1, -1) = 2.35619   (135 deg)
atan2(-1, -1) = -2.35619   (-135 deg)

atan cannot tell the quadrants apart:
atan( 1 /  1) = 0.785398
atan(-1 / -1) = 0.785398   (same value!)
